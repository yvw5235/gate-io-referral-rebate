## What a Gate referral code actually changes

Gate's referral system ties two accounts together. The person who invited you earns a commission calculated from the trading fees your account pays, and Gate lets that person route part of that commission back to you as a fee discount. That second half is the part you care about.

So the code isn't a coupon for a fixed dollar amount, and it isn't a signup bonus in the usual sense. It's a permanent link between your account and the inviter's, which then determines how much of your fee spend comes back.

### The 20% cap is the number that actually matters

Code-hosting pages advertise fee rebates of 15% and 20%, and Gate's own documentation explains why nobody advertises 30%. The cap on what an inviter can share back is **20%** — of spot trading commission, and 20% for any futures discount configured on the link. If the inviter's own commission rate is lower than 20%, they can pass on all of it. Either way, you're never looking at a third of your fees coming back.

Two more limits worth knowing, because they explain why every code page pushes spot volume:

- Spot and margin commission only accrues on your trades while you're at Regular through VIP 2. Hit VIP 3 and the inviter's commission stops — which is also when the rebate structure becomes irrelevant to you anyway, since VIP 3 rates are already lower.
- Futures commission only accrues from Regular and VIP 1 users, and only for 12 months from the date the futures account is opened.

One quirk that trips people up: Gate's own wiki states that a futures discount share of up to 20% can be configured on a referral link, but it also states that invitees currently don't receive discounts on their own futures trades. Those two lines sit awkwardly together. Practically, treat spot as the line item that pays, and check the referral page under your account for what your specific link has switched on.

## Things that quietly void a referral reward

Worth a scan before you deposit anything:

- Registering without the code, then asking support to attach it. Gate doesn't do retroactive binding on exchange accounts.
- Deleting and recreating an account to get a different code. Both accounts lose eligibility.
- Using identity documents already tied to another Gate account. Commissions get cut and rebates stop.
- Depositing through a card channel or transferring GateCode. Neither counts as a qualifying deposit for the campaign tasks.
- Reactivating the campaign but completing only one of the two required friend boosts. The round fails for everyone.
- Letting the voucher sit. Five days for the invite campaign, fourteen for the other one.
- Assuming futures rebates are being paid to you. Under the current terms described in Gate's wiki, they aren't.
- Living in a restricted region. The invite campaigns don't run there at all.

## Short answers to the questions people actually ask

**Can I add the code after I've registered?** No. It's a one-shot field. If you've already got an account without one, opening a second account with matching KYC won't help and will cost you eligibility.

**Does the code cost me anything?** No. The rebate comes out of the inviter's commission, not your balance, and it doesn't reduce any of Gate's advertised benefits.

**How much is the rebate, in plain terms?** Whatever the inviter chose to share, capped at 20% of spot commission. Third-party codes commonly advertise 15–20%. Anyone promising more than 20% is describing something other than the referral rebate.

**Do I need to verify my identity?** You can create an account without it, but the campaign tasks require KYC, and the campaigns carry deadlines — one affiliate walkthrough puts the verification window at 15 days from registration, after which unreleased new-user rewards are forfeited. Verify early rather than discovering the deadline later.

**Does the code work on the app?** The exchange referral system covers web and app. If you register through the app, type the code in manually — and note that the Web3 wallet is a different signup flow with its own 7-day binding window.

**Is a bigger code better than the one in front of me?** Only in the sense that the inviter's sharing setting determines your rebate. Everything else — the fee schedule, the VIP thresholds, the campaign pool — is identical for every code on the platform. If the difference between two codes is a few percentage points of a 0.1% fee, it's a rounding error next to whether you enable GT deduction and stop paying 0.1% in the first place.
